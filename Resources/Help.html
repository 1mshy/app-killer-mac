Killer

Find the process that won’t quit. Stop it. See what happened.

Force quit an app

  1. Choose Applications and search by name, process ID, or executable path.
  2. Select the app and click Force Quit.
  3. Confirm the named process. Unsaved changes may be lost.

Killer verifies that the selected process exited, then checks for a replacement using the same executable for two seconds. Results stay in Recent activity for this session. No process is stopped automatically.

Background processes and administrator access

Use Background to find third-party helpers, daemons, and command-line processes. A process owned by another user requires Force Quit as Administrator. macOS handles authorization; Killer never reads or stores a password. The app’s one-shot helper checks the process identity again immediately before the stop request. It installs no background service.

When an app comes back

Apps like FortiClient have separate interface, helper, and security processes. Stopping one process does not stop the others. A background service can restart it, even after Killer’s short check has finished. Select any helper explicitly; Killer never stops a whole family by guessing a name.

Administrator access cannot override macOS protection or a managed app’s policy. Use the vendor’s supported shutdown procedure or contact your administrator. Killer does not disable startup services, uninstall software, or change system protection.
